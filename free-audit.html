What's covered

A Real Audit, Not a Template

Every review is done manually against your specific business — not run through a generic SEO tool and emailed back as a PDF.

SEO & Search Visibility

Are you showing up when potential clients search for your services in your area? We check your meta structure, local signals, Google Business Profile alignment, and on-page SEO.

Lead Capture Effectiveness

Does your site actually convert visitors into inquiries? We look at your CTAs, form placement, friction points, and what's blocking potential clients from contacting you.

Page Speed & Performance

A slow site loses visitors before they read anything. We check load time, Core Web Vitals, image optimization, and mobile responsiveness.

Tech Stack Review

What tools are you currently running? Which ones are redundant, which ones are missing, and which ones are creating more work than they're saving? We map it out.

Operations Gap Analysis

Where is your team spending time on manual work that could be automated or systemized? We identify the highest-friction points in your day-to-day operations.

Prioritized Action List

Not a list of 40 things to fix. A ranked, specific breakdown of what to do first for the highest ROI — organized by effort and impact so you know where to start.
