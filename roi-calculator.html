How the Math Works

Every number above comes from three inputs and arithmetic you can check yourself.

Monthly cost
Employees × admin hours per week × hourly rate × 4.33 weeks. That last figure is the average number of weeks in a month, not four — using four understates the cost by about 8%.
Annual cost
Employees × admin hours per week × hourly rate × 52 weeks. This is the number worth sitting with — it is usually larger than people expect, because manual work is spread thin enough that nobody sees the total.
Payback period
$15,000 — the entry point for a V1 operations build — divided by your monthly cost. Bigger scopes push the payback out proportionally, and it assumes the software removes most, not all, of the manual work. Your actual scope determines the real figure, and we put that in writing before any code is written.
This is an estimate, not a quote. It is meant to tell you whether the conversation is worth having — not to price your project.
