BK Toolbox Custom Home Designs

A full migration off Wix onto a fully owned, custom-coded platform — domain and all — purpose-built around two distinct service lines and independently verified at 100 SEO and 99 performance on Google PageSpeed.

The Problem

A craftsmanship business with a web presence working against it

BK Toolbox owned their domain — bktoolbox.com — but it was tied to a templated Wix site: a single generic page with no dedicated structure for their two distinct service lines, interior design and home remodeling, and locked into Wix's platform, hosting, and monthly plan.

Image alt text was left as raw iPhone filenames (IMG_3995.heic), there was no local SEO targeting, and the whole presence was boxed in by what the template would allow. For a business built on craftsmanship and attention to detail, the site was undercutting that reputation, not reinforcing it.

The Rebuild

A fully custom platform they own outright

We handled the full migration end-to-end — domain, Google Workspace email, and the website itself — moving everything off Wix onto a self-managed VPS the client fully owns. No more platform lock-in, no third-party branding, no subscription required to keep the site online.

Before
  • Domain, email & website all tied to Wix
  • One flat page mixing both service lines
  • No local SEO or city-level targeting
  • Raw iPhone filenames left in image alt text
  • Generic Wix contact widget for every request
  • Boxed in by template & hosting limits
After
  • Custom-coded Next.js site on a dedicated VPS — domain, email & site migrated intact
  • Two service verticals, each with its own overview, portfolio & quote flow
  • City-specific landing pages for local SEO across Utah
  • Descriptive, keyword-rich alt text on every image
  • Rotating hero photography with per-service video previews
  • Live Instagram feed + trust bar (licensed & insured)

Full technical SEO shipped with the build: structured meta descriptions, Open Graph and Twitter card data, canonical tags, and descriptive alt text across every image — the on-page signals that let a site actually get found.
