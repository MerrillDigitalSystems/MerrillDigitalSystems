BrightPath Dental Center

The Problem

A practice running on scattered documents and disconnected tools

BrightPath Dental Center needed one internal home for how the practice actually runs — announcements, role-based permissions, location verification, HR, training, and compliance — instead of information spread across shared drives, email threads, and generic apps that never fit the way the team works.

The requirement was a custom internal system scoped precisely to their process — with the right people seeing the right things — and deliverable on a defined timeline without the uncertainty that often derails internal-software projects.

The Build

Scoped, built, and shipped by one person

The project was scoped in a single discovery session — no drawn-out discovery phase, no committee of project managers. Every requirement was documented before the build started, which meant the timeline held because the scope was locked.

Built on Zoho Creator with Deluge, the system centralizes the practice's internal pages behind role-based access, so admins, managers, and staff each see exactly what they should. The same person BrightPath spoke with on day one built it from start to finish — context carried across the entire project, so decisions made in week one didn't have to be re-explained later.

Company Directory Zoho Creator Deluge Role-Based Access HR & Compliance
The Outcome

Delivered on schedule. 5.0 across the board.

The project was completed and delivered on the agreed timeline at the agreed cost. BrightPath's review on Clutch — verified by the platform — rated the engagement 5.0 across all three categories: quality, schedule, and cost.

The system is in active use across the practice, giving the team one reliable place for the information and processes they rely on every day.
